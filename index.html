Our Approach

Our Approach

Our learning approach is grounded in internationally recognized curricula, integrating the Singapore and Cambridge frameworks to build strong academic foundations and future-ready skills.

01
➗

CPA (Concrete–Pictorial–Abstract) for Primary Mathematics

We apply the CPA approach to Primary Mathematics, guiding students from hands-on learning with concrete materials, to visual representations, and finally to abstract problem-solving.

Concrete
Students explore concepts using hands-on materials and real-life examples.
Pictorial
Ideas are reinforced through visual models, diagrams, and representations.
Abstract
Students confidently apply symbols, numbers, and formal problem-solving strategies.
This approach helps students truly understand concepts, not just memorize answers.
02
📝

Personalized Worksheet

Each student receives comprehensive worksheets tailored to their ability and learning pace, aligned with their school's topic.

Tailored Content
Worksheets customized to each student's ability level.
School Aligned
Topics aligned with their school's curriculum.
Test Preparation
Preparation for quizzes, tests, and final assignments.
We ensure every student learns at their optimal pace.
03
📊

Bar Model Problem Solving

Word problems are taught using the bar model strategy, helping students visualize relationships and solve problems logically and systematically.

Visual Learning
Students visualize mathematical relationships.
Logical Thinking
Develop systematic problem-solving skills.
Confidence
Build confidence in tackling complex word problems.
Making math accessible and understandable for everyone.
